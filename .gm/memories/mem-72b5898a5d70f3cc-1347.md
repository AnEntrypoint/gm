gm performance measurement is invalid unless BOTH conditions hold: the host is verified quiet, and the A/B comparison is interleaved A/B/A/B rather than sequential A-then-B. Witnessed failing three separate ways in one session. A sequential before-then-after on rs-codeinsight fabricated an apparent 3x REGRESSION from a change that was really a 10-40% improvement; the artifact vanished entirely under interleaving on identical cache state. A parent session measured instruction at 38s and codesearch at 57s and filed two confident wrong diagnoses in sequence (daemon serialization, then head-of-line blocking) before controlled retest showed both were host CPU saturation from its own concurrent subagent cargo builds; 6 concurrent dispatches actually complete in 560ms total and a cheap verb behind an in-flight codesearch returns in 253ms. A third effort had intermediate numbers confounded by sibling-session load and by its own benchmark files backing up in the spool in-dir. Practical rule: report medians over 10 or more interleaved pairs, never a single pair, and treat any measurement taken while subagents are running builds as void. Corollary that cost real time here: when fanning out profiling subagents, the parent must stop issuing its own timed dispatches, because its load corrupts their measurements and theirs corrupts its own.
