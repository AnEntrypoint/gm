Windows console-suppression for any spawn in the gm/agentplug stack needs three layers together, not one or two: (1) windowsHide:true (STARTF_USESHOWWINDOW+SW_HIDE) hides only the immediate child's window; (2) resolve bare commands (bun/node/git) to their real .exe via `where` to bypass .cmd shims that re-enter conhost with fresh unhidden defaults; (3) creationFlags:0x08000000 (CREATE_NO_WINDOW), the load-bearing fix -- this flag is INHERITED by the entire descendant process tree, so no process below the spawn point can ever allocate a console window. Combine with DETACHED_PROCESS (0x00000008) for daemon spawns: creationFlags:0x08000008. Layer 3 is what stops a multi-level chain (bun-x downloading and launching a .cmd shim that spawns node.exe with fresh defaults) from popping consoles even when the top-level parent is hidden. windowsHide:true alone or windowsHide+where-resolution alone are both insufficient -- witnessed real regression where layers 1+2 shipped but conhost still flashed until layer 3 landed. On POSIX, Node ignores creationFlags, safe to set unconditionally.
