rs-plugkit Policy struct field rationale (fsm.rs, gm's graph.json policy object): longgap_refresh_verbs -- verbs whose dispatch RESETS the long-gap clock (.gm/last-instruction-ts), the third member alongside exempt/penalized verb lists and the only one previously unconfigurable: exempt verbs are not penalised by the clock, whereas refresh verbs actively renew it. A project renaming or adding an orienting verb needs this configurable or that verb dispatches forever without ever clearing the long-gap denial it keeps triggering.
