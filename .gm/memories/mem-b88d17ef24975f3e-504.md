Subprocess pipe deadlock in host bridges: Command with Stdio::piped followed by wait_timeout and only then read_to_end hangs whenever the child writes more than the 64KB pipe buffer; host_git on a 1.7MB git show sat at 0% CPU for the full 300s git timeout and held the gm Store the whole time (codesearch took 347s). Drain stdout and stderr on their own threads from spawn (subprocess.rs PipeDrain) and join them after wait_timeout; the same shape existed in exec_js, the CDP helper and background tasks.
