rs-plugkit Policy.deviation_severity field rationale: per-deviation-kind severity override, keyed by a name in orchestrator::deviations::DEVIATION_TABLE, valued "deny" or "log". Empty by default so every kind keeps the registry's declared severity and shipped behavior is unchanged. A project promotes a log-only kind (unsolicited-doc-created, prd-anti-shape, platform-search-drift, spool-poll, complete-chain-poll, synthetic-test-file) to a hard denial by naming it here, or demotes a denying one where the workflow legitimately differs. Unknown keys and unparseable values fall back to the registry default and surface as a non-fatal warning rather than silently weakening a gate.
