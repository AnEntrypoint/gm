rs-plugkit HookOutcome enum design rationale: every gate-hook outcome previously collapsed to a bare false, so an operator staring at a denial could not tell a MISSING hook file from a hook that ran and legitimately said no -- one is a broken config, the other is the system working, and they demand opposite responses but looked identical. Fixed by an explicit HookOutcome enum (Passed, Missing, ExecFailed, ReturnedFalse, Unsupported) with a reason() method producing operator-facing text distinguishing each case, appended to the gate's own denial message.
