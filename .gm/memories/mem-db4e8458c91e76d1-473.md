rs-plugkit Policy.shell_verbs field rationale: verbs treated as "a shell" for the shell-bypass check, and the tool that bypass is steering people toward. Hardcoding these made the rule un-vendorable -- a workflow whose shell verb is named something else got no protection at all, and one that legitimately wants shell git had no way to say so. Kept as policy rather than a compile-time constant because "which verb is a shell" is a property of the workflow, not the engine.
