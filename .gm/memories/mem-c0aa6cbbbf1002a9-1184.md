The fsm-propose-override kind:prose content-shape rule lives in gm-config/prose/entry.md under a Self-reconfiguration content shape section (served to every project on every turn), not AGENTS.md alone -- AGENTS.md is gm-repo-local and never loads when gm drives other projects, so a universal-reach rule belongs in served config, with only a short pointer sentence left in AGENTS.md. The rule itself: every fsm-propose-override proposal of kind:"prose" expresses named-technique content as attributed anchors (name+author, e.g. MECE (Barbara Minto)), never paraphrase. Where multiple anchors relate, express the relation as a mermaid flowchart with -.-> edges, never a flat list. Never invent an edge -- only a relation genuinely known from the cited literature (shared author, documented dependency, explicit see-also). If synthesis surfaces a well-known attributed technique missing from the target phase's anchor list, add it with correct attribution and graph it in. Never cite an external anchor-catalog website by name or URL; the anchor+author pair is the self-contained citation. Applies to every prose-kind self-reconfiguration proposal on every project, not a one-time pass.
