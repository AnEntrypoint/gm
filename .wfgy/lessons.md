Generalizes to: When a shared multi-project daemon is genuinely saturated by ANOTHER session's long-running operation (not a bug in this session's own dispatch), dispatch_orphaned repeating 3-4x with a changing pid and watcher.log showing sustained "database is locked" from a different sess= tag is diagnostic of contention, not of a dead/broken watcher -- do not reboot (that would interrupt the other session's real work) and do not endlessly retry (that adds load to an already-saturated resource). The correct move is: register the contention itself as a PRD row (it is real, fixable, in-tree work), apply bounded retry, then surface/checkpoint rather than loop forever waiting on someone else's lock.
