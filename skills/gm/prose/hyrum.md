# Hyrum's Law - Hyrum Wright

Standing tension `HYRUM`. A cost the method accepts, not a problem it solves. Entered from any neighbour that leads here; left to any neighbour it links to. Its place in a walk is set by the work it applies to, not a bucket. When it fires, take a local exception and record the reason, not reverse the method.

The walk is recorded by the transition that leaves this node.
