# Programming as Theory Building - Peter Naur

Standing tension `NAURTHEORY`. A cost the method accepts, not a problem it solves. Entered from any neighbour that leads here, left to any neighbour it links to; its place in a walk is decided by the work it applies to, not by a bucket. When it fires, make a local exception and record its reason; the method stays.

The walk is recorded by the transition that leaves this node.
