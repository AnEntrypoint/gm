# Silent Spec-Code Drift - Grabowski

Standing tension `SPECDRIFT`. A cost the method accepts, not a problem it solves. Entered from any neighbour that leads here, left to any neighbour it links to; its place in a walk is decided by the work it applies to, not by a bucket. When it fires, take a local exception and record the reason rather than reversing the method.

The walk is recorded by the transition that leaves this node.
