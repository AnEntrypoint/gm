# Silent Spec-Code Drift - Grabowski

Standing tension `SPECDRIFT`. A cost the method accepts, not a problem it solves. Entered from any neighbour that leads here, left to any neighbour it links to; the work it applies to decides its place in a walk, not a bucket. On firing, take a local exception, record its reason, and keep the method.

The walk is recorded by the transition that leaves this node.
